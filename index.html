THIRA: Women-Centred Tech and Ideas Conclave in Kochi

THIRA is a technology and ideas conclave in Kochi, Kerala, India, brought to you by IEEE MEC Student Branch WIE.

About THIRA

THIRA creates an inclusive space to listen, question, engage and discover new perspectives across technology, careers, leadership, innovation, creativity and the journeys of women choosing their own paths.

Programme themes

  • Emerging technology and technical fields
  • Careers, leadership and personal journeys
  • Lightning talks and keynote conversations
  • Panels, plenary sessions and community experiences

Location and audience

THIRA is based in Kochi, also known as Cochin, Kerala, India, and welcomes attendees from Kochi, across Kerala and throughout South India. Everyone who wants to listen, participate and learn is welcome.
